# What the evidence says about content that AI answers use

Each point gives its source and date. These systems change often; use the newest source available, and present each finding as what was measured, where and when.

## Google, on its own AI features

- "Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search", updated 10 July 2026. Foundational SEO still applies, because AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on Google's ranking systems. Create non-commodity content: a unique point of view, first-hand experience, organised with clear headings. Do not split content into chunks or rewrite it just for AI; creating content for every query variation to manipulate answers violates the scaled content abuse policy. To appear in these features, a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet, and the site must be included in the Search generative AI setting in Search Console. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## Studies of what AI answers cite

- Aggarwal et al., "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization", KDD 2024. On their benchmark of generative engine answers, adding statistics, quotations and cited sources to a page raised its visibility in the answers by about 30 to 40%; keyword stuffing did not help. A lab benchmark, not a measurement of any live assistant. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735
- Ahrefs, 17 million citations, July 2025: pages AI assistants cite were 25.7% newer on average than Google's organic results; ChatGPT leaned most towards newer pages. https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/
- Ahrefs, 75,000 brands, May 2025: the strongest factor linked to brands appearing in Google's AI Overviews was how often other sites mention them (correlation 0.664). A correlation, not proof of cause. https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/
- Ahrefs, AI search overlap: only 8% of the pages ChatGPT cited were in Google's top 10 for the same question; Perplexity's overlap was higher, 28.6%. https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/
- SE Ranking: Microsoft Copilot shared only 9.8% of its cited sites with Google's AI Overviews. https://seranking.com/blog/chatgpt-vs-perplexity-vs-google-vs-bing-comparison-research/

## What follows from it

- Write for people, with a clear answer, real evidence and the brand's own experience.
- Keep important pages current and show the date.
- Being mentioned on other credible sites matters alongside the brand's own pages; earn it with something worth citing, never with fake mentions.
- Each assistant finds sources its own way, so a high Google position alone does not decide what an assistant cites.
