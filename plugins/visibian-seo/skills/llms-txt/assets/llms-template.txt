# Example Co

> Example Co makes scheduling software for dental clinics in the US and Canada, sold by monthly subscription.

Prices on this site are in US dollars and include all features of each plan. The documentation covers the web app and the API.

## Product

- [Home](https://example.com/): what the software does and who it is for
- [Pricing](https://example.com/pricing): the three plans, their prices and limits
- [Integrations](https://example.com/integrations): the practice management systems it connects to

## Documentation

- [Getting started](https://docs.example.com/start): set up a clinic in five steps
- [API reference](https://docs.example.com/api): endpoints, authentication and rate limits

## Company

- [Contact](https://example.com/contact): support and sales addresses
