# Stages of the decision

| Stage | What the buyer asks | Example | What answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem | How to solve a problem, without naming a category yet | How do small clinics stop patients missing appointments? | A guide that explains the options honestly |
| Category | Which products of a kind fit their situation | What is the best scheduling software for a two-chair dental clinic? | A product or category page that says plainly who it is for |
| Comparison | One brand against another, or alternatives to one | Acme vs Example: which is better for small clinics? Alternatives to Example | A fair comparison page (see the `comparison-pages` skill) |
| Decision | Price, trust, fit, risk | How much does Acme cost for two dentists? Does Acme work with Dentrix? | Pricing, integrations, reviews, security and policy pages |
| Use | Setup and how-to, after or just before buying | How do I import patients into Acme? | Documentation and help pages |

Questions at the category and comparison stages invite a list of brands in the answer. Make sure each of them has a page that answers it in plain words.
